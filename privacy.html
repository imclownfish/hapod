Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

What HAPOD stores

Without an account, HAPOD stores your workout completion count, streak, selected language, theme, sound preference, and unlocked workout days only in your browser on your device.

When you choose Google sign-in, HAPOD uses Firebase Authentication to sign you in and stores your completed-session count, streak, and last completed date in Cloud Firestore so they can sync across your devices. HAPOD does not copy your Google email address or name into its workout-statistics database.

What HAPOD does not do

HAPOD does not use device fingerprinting, sell personal information, collect precise location, collect contacts, or ask for a phone number.

Service providers

Google Firebase provides authentication and the database used for signed-in account syncing. Google handles account sign-in under its own privacy policy. HAPOD does not currently use advertising trackers or sell personal information.

Your choices

You can clear local HAPOD data by clearing this website's browser storage. This removes the local streak and completed-session count from that device. To request deletion of synced workout data or your HAPOD account data, email the address below from the account you used to sign in.

Health notice

HAPOD provides general exercise information, not medical advice. Talk with a qualified health professional before starting or changing an exercise program, especially if you have an injury, medical condition, or concern.
