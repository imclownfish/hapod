Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

What HAPOD stores today

HAPOD currently works without an account. Your workout completion count, streak, selected language, theme, sound preference, and unlocked workout days are stored only in your browser on your device. HAPOD does not currently send this information to a HAPOD server.

What HAPOD does not do

HAPOD does not use device fingerprinting, sell personal information, collect precise location, collect contacts, or ask for a phone number.

Future accounts

Accounts and cross-device sync are not live yet. Before they launch, this policy will be updated to explain exactly what account information is collected, how workout statistics are stored, the service providers involved, and how to delete an account and its data. An optional import from a device's local workout history will be clearly shown before it happens.

Your choices

You can clear HAPOD's current local data by clearing this website's browser storage. This also removes your local streak and completed-session count from that device.

Health notice

HAPOD provides general exercise information, not medical advice. Talk with a qualified health professional before starting or changing an exercise program, especially if you have an injury, medical condition, or concern.
